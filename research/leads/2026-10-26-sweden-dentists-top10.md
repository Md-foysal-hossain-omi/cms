# Sweden: first 10 leads (owner-run dental clinics with outdated websites)

Date: 2026-10-26. Method: 85 private clinics collected from varden.se in 12 mid-size cities,
43 websites opened and screenshotted on mobile and desktop (`tools/lead-finder/`), then judged by eye.

## Plan

**Niche:** owner-run private dentists. The dentist owns the clinic and decides alone. Dentistry is
high-value per patient, and private clinics compete with Folktandvården for new patients, so a
website that wins bookings is worth money to them.

**Cities:** mid-size towns (Västerås, Örebro, Gävle, Sundsvall, Eskilstuna, Karlstad, Kalmar, Borås,
Halmstad, Växjö, Jönköping, Falun). Stockholm, Göteborg and Malmö are full of local agencies.

**Excluded on purpose:**
- Praktikertjänst clinics (sites say "Om Praktikertjänst"): the group controls the template, so the dentist cannot buy a new site. About 10 of the 43 sites.
- Chains: Folktandvården, Smile, Dentsmile, Colosseum, Tandläkargruppen, Happident, Vesalis.
- Sites that already look modern.
- Cura Dental / peterschulz.se (Västerås): new owner in 2026 and a new site already built.

**Next niches with the same method:** physiotherapists, naprapaths and chiropractors, small vet clinics,
opticians, solo law firms, accounting firms (varden.se covers the health ones).

## Outreach

- Write in **Swedish**. Short, personal, from you, no attachments in the first email.
- First email: one sentence on what you saw (a real detail from the table), one mockup image link, one question ("Får jag skicka över ett förslag?").
- Two follow-ups, 4 and 10 days later. A phone call works well with Swedish clinics too.
- Price anchor for a small clinic site: about 15,000 to 30,000 SEK, plus optional monthly care.

**Legal (Sweden):** emailing an AB or HB at its company address is generally fine for B2B marketing,
with a clear opt-out in every email. A sole trader (enskild firma) counts as a private person, and
marketing email to them needs prior consent. For those, call or send a letter instead. Check each
lead's company form on allabolag.se before emailing.
