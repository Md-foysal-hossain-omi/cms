## Before you press Send

1. Open the proposal on your own phone once.
2. Check the jobs page again on the send day. If the jobs are gone, delete the sentence "Ni söker fem nya kollegor ..." and send without it.
3. Send Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00 to 10:00 Swedish time.
4. Do not send on the same day as lead 1 and 2 from a new address: max 2 to 3 cold emails per day for the first week.
