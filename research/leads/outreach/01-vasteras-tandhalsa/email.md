## Why it is built this way

| Choice | Reason |
|---|---|
| Subject "Omar?" | Looks like a personal note, not marketing. High open rate. |
| One link, one image | Spam filters punish many links and attachments. |
| Two real problems from his own site | Proves you actually looked, builds trust in 5 seconds. |
| "exakt samma innehåll" | He has no work to do: no new texts, no photos. |
| Yes/no question | Easy to answer from a phone. |
| Opt-out line | Required for B2B email marketing in Sweden, and it builds trust. |
| Links only in the signature | Shows a real person with real profiles without crowding the message. |

## Before you press Send

1. Open all signature links once yourself (LinkedIn and Upwork block my checks, so I could not test them).
2. Send one test to mail-tester.com from the same address. Send to Omar only at score 9/10 or higher.
3. Open the proposal link on your own phone.
4. Send Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00 to 10:00 Swedish time.
5. If you move the proposal to `forslag.foysalsolutions.com`, replace the link first (better for spam filters).
