# Tandläkarmottagning Ekersgatan 7 (Örebro): design plan

Brief: same content as tandlakareekersgatan.com (21 pages on WordPress.com), premium look with animation.
Today: a free WordPress.com theme with a 25-item text menu, a stock palm-beach photo as header on every page
and a "Drivs med WordPress.com" footer.
Audience: adults on Väster in Örebro, people with acute pain, and Flyg & Dyk staff (FM, Kustbevakning,
Räddningstjänst, Polis, Tull). Primary jobs: book online (Opus Dental, open around the clock) or call.
Subject: dentist Matti Koivuniemi. Long opening hours, street-level entrance, near Resecentrum, full
reference price list with treatment codes on 12 pages, and his own saying: "Tandhälsan är som ett banklån".

## Tokens
- Color: Gata #F2F4F3 (page), Vit #FFFFFF, Ram #17463A (the dark green frame of their door sign; dark bands),
  Skylt #23899A (the teal letters on the sign; links and buttons), Signal #F3B23A (only for "akut"),
  Bläck #13201D (text).
- Type: Archivo only. Display at width 112–125 (expanded) weight 700 for a sign-like, practical voice; body at
  normal width 400/500. Sentence case.

## The memorable thing
"Vad kostar det?": the whole reference price list (over 60 codes from 12 pages) in one place, with a search
field ("sök på behandling eller kod") and category chips. Rows filter live and the matching count animates.
It turns 12 thin pages into one tool that answers the most common question.

## Other motion, each answering something
- Hero headline rises, building photo settles, "Öppet nu / Stängt" chip from Swedish time.
- The banklån quote: "skyhög ränta" gets a marker underline drawn when it scrolls into view.
- Hours: today's row highlighted.
- "Bra att veta" accordion for the longer texts (rotbehandling, bettskena, proteser, tandsten).
- Sections fade up once; reduced motion turns it all off.
