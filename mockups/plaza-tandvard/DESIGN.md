# Plaza Tandvård AB (Borås): design plan

Brief: same content as plazatandvard.se, premium look with animation.
Today: an Elementor site by an agency, with fresh 2026 content (Invisalign price table, 10-tandvården page, a job
ad), but a dull black-and-white stock hero, "© 2021" in the footer, a cookie bar over the phone screen and a
generic look that hides the clinic's best assets. **Risk:** they already pay an agency and update often, so this
is a harder sell than leads 1–6. Pitch quality and Invisalign conversion, not "your site is broken".
Audience: adults in Borås, Invisalign candidates from 15 years, people 67+ (10-tandvården 2026).
Subject: two dentist teams, Team Wagner (Malin Wagner) and Team Storvik (Andreas Storvik, who also lectures other
dentists in Invisalign), plus Docent Peter Nilsson, oral surgeon. Allégatan 11–15, floor 2.
Real assets: a 2025 group photo (charcoal scrubs, lime-green collars), three portraits, a 3D-scanner photo.

## Tokens
- Color: Kol #23282B (header, dark bands: the scrubs), Lime #9BCB3C (the scrub collar; one accent, used for
  focus and highlights only), Grön #5F8F1F (accessible green for buttons and links on light), Ljus #F5F6F3
  (page), Vit #FFFFFF, Grafit #4F575B (secondary text).
- Type: Urbanist 600/700 for headings, Karla 400/500 for text. Sentence case.

## The memorable thing
"Hitta rätt Invisalign-nivå": a four-way switch (Go, Lite, Moderate, Comprehensive) that swaps in the real data
from their comparison table (passar ofta för, antal skenor, behandlingstid, efterjusteringar, pris). A bar shows
the number of aligners out of 99 and the price counts to the new value.

## Second tool
10-tandvården: "Vilket år är du född?" answers whether the strengthened high-cost protection applies this year
(it applies from the calendar year you turn 67), using only the rule on their own page.

## Other motion
- Hero headline rises, group photo settles; sections fade up once; reduced motion turns it off.
