# Tandläkarna Norenius HB (Västerås): design plan

Brief: same content as norenius.se, premium look with animation.
Today: a CMS.SE template with an 8-item uppercase menu, a grey-tinted hero photo, a Cookiebot box that covers
most of the phone screen, no online booking and **no email address anywhere** (only phone 021-12 52 53).
Audience: adults in central Västerås (listing from the year you turn 20). Primary job: call during phone hours.
Subject: family-run private clinic by Stora torget. Two dentists (David Norenius, Sandra Norenius Sundqvist),
two hygienists, three dental nurses. Owners: Göran Norenius AB and Sandra Norenius AB (partnership HB).
Real assets: a professional photo shoot on white (group + 7 portraits in turquoise scrubs), two warm
waiting-room photos, treatment and panorama X-ray photos. Script logo "Tandläkarna Norenius" in light cyan.

## Tokens
- Color: Vit #FFFFFF (page; portraits are shot on white so they melt into it), Is #EAF7F9 (soft panels),
  Turkos #12A6BA (the scrubs; buttons and highlights), Djup #0D2A33 (text and the dark band),
  Linne #F3EEE6 (from the waiting-room sofas; one warm band).
- Type: Instrument Serif for display (elegant, echoes the script logo), Hanken Grotesk 400/500/600 for text.
  Sentence case.

## The memorable thing
"Ring oss nu?": the clinic books only by phone, so the hero card shows live, in Swedish time, whether the
phone line is open right now and until when, today's phone hours, and a big call button. Outside phone
hours it says when the line opens next. It turns two hour tables in the footer into the one answer people need.

## Other motion
- Team line-up: the seven portraits rise in one by one like the group photo, names under each.
- Treatments: list on the left that follows your scroll (scroll-spy) while the texts scroll on the right.
- Hero headline rises; group photo settles; sections fade up once. Reduced motion turns it all off.
