# Tandhälsan i Eskilstuna: design plan

Brief: same content as tandhalsaneskilstuna.se (17 pages), premium look with animation.
Audience: families and adults in central Eskilstuna. Primary job: call 016-13 33 55 or mail.
Subject: owner-run general practice (Tigran Capital AB, owner David Karapetjan), Careliigatan 9A,
more than 4 100 listed patients, about 8 staff. Their own photo shoot: team in teal-blue scrubs on the
jetty by Eskilstunaån, the twin towers of Klosters kyrka behind them, plus warm photos of children
in the chair. Logo: bright cyan family-in-a-tooth mark.

## Tokens
- Color: Morgon #F6F8F7 (page), Papper #FFFFFF (cards), Ån #0B3B4C (deep river, dark bands and text
  headings), Logga #1592C8 (their logo cyan, links and focus), Scrubs #0E7C8C (buttons, the team's
  uniform), Tulpan #F4E3A1 (the yellow tulips in their waiting room photo, one warm accent: the cost
  calculator), Bläck #14232A (text).
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 600/700 for headings (friendly, a little playful, fits a family clinic),
  Figtree 400/500/600 for body. Sentence case, no all-caps labels.
- Shapes: generous 28px radius on photos and cards, pill buttons. A soft wave line (the river) as the
  only ornament, used twice.

## The memorable thing
"Vad betalar jag?": a slider for the treatment's reference price that shows, live, how the state high-cost
protection on their own "Vad kostar det?" page splits it: up to 3 000 kr you pay, 3 001–15 000 kr you pay
half, over 15 000 kr you pay 15 %. A stacked bar fills in three colours and the total counts up. It turns
their densest paragraph into something a patient understands in two seconds.

## Other motion, each answering something
- Hero: team photo settles from 1.1 to 1 scale, headline lines rise once, "Öppet nu" chip.
- Facts strip: numbers count up once (4 100 patients, 8 staff, 19 years free care).
- Treatments: nine-item picker (list left, panel right on desktop; chips over panel on mobile), panel
  fades between treatments. Undersökning shows its five steps as a real ordered list.
- Team: portraits rise in a stagger.
- Hours: today's row highlighted, Friday row says whether this week is even (open 8–13) or odd (closed),
  computed from the ISO week in Swedish time.
- Reduced motion: all of it off, final states shown.

## Checked against defaults
Not cream + serif + terracotta. Different from leads 1–3 (navy grotesk, river-dark serif, lime-white sea
blue). No eyebrow labels, no 01/02 markers except the examination steps, which are a real sequence.
